Get Your Wheels Rolling Smoothly

Keeping your tyres in top condition is essential for a safe and smooth driving experience. Our Tyres & Wheel Services ensure that your vehicle’s tyres are fitted, balanced, and aligned for maximum performance and safety. Whether you need brand-new tyres, reliable used options, or precision alignment, we have you covered.

Our Services

  • New Car Tyres – From £50: Budget, mid-range, and premium tyres with expert fitting.
  • Used Car Tyres – From £25: Cost-effective, safety-checked used tyres for all makes and models.
  • Wheel Alignment – From £40: Corrects misaligned wheels for even tyre wear and better handling.
  • Wheel Balancing – From £12.50: Eliminates vibrations and ensures smooth driving.
  • Puncture Repair – From £15: Quick, affordable tyre repairs to get you back on the road.
  • TPMS Repair & Service – From £20: Sensor replacements, programming, and TPMS diagnostics.
